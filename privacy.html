Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At PostAlchemy, we take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our AI content generation tool.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Our tool is built with a "privacy-first" approach. When you enter text into the generator, it is processed in real-time and is not stored in any database. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal details.

2. Third-Party AI Services

To generate your content, we use the Google Gemini API. Your input text is sent to Google's servers for processing. We recommend reviewing Google's Privacy Policy to understand how they handle API data.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We may use basic cookies to improve your experience and track website performance (like page views). We do not use aggressive tracking or sell your data to third parties.

4. Google AdSense

We may use third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads. These companies may use cookies to display relevant ads based on your visits to this and other websites.
